SQL — validation layer

All KPIs are first validated in SQL before DAX. See sql/02_kpi_queries.sql and sql/03_advanced_analysis.sql.

-- YoY example
WITH monthly AS (
  SELECT date_trunc('month', order_date) AS m, SUM(sales) AS sales
  FROM fact_sales GROUP BY 1
)
SELECT m, sales,
  LAG(sales,12) OVER (ORDER BY m) AS last_year,
  (sales - LAG(sales,12) OVER (ORDER BY m)) / NULLIF(LAG(sales,12) OVER (ORDER BY m),0) AS yoy_pct
FROM monthly;

Insights & Recommendations

  • West leads profit, Central leaks margin — Central margin -4.2 pts vs avg despite 22% of sales.

  • Q4 volume up, margin down — holiday discounting; Q1 is most profitable per dollar.
